## 👤 About Me

I am a **Senior Data Platform Engineer and Architect** with a track record of designing and delivering production-grade data systems across financial services and industrial IoT. Currently at **BrightSource Energy**, I lead the architecture of **IoT-driven Data Lakehouses** on AWS to optimize real-time renewable energy operations at scale.

My career spans quantitative risk and fraud engineering at tier-1 financial institutions (**JPMorgan Chase, Citi**) through to real-time industrial telemetry — giving me a rare breadth across regulated financial domains and high-throughput engineering environments.

I specialize in the **Modern Data Stack**: building **open-format Lakehouses** with **Apache Iceberg** and **Apache Flink** that unify streaming and batch workloads under a single, scalable architecture. Beyond backend systems, I build full-stack developer tools, AI-assisted platforms, and interactive prep guides — treating data infrastructure with the same engineering rigor as application software.
